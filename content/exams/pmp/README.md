# Project management practice content

As of 15 November 2026, this package has four topics and twelve original questions: stakeholders, risk basics, predictive change control, and Scrum delivery. Each topic assesses three explicit ideas. These are short learning exercises, not a complete PMP syllabus or a validated readiness assessment. The change-control scenarios explicitly use a predictive project and its designated authority; they do not prescribe a review board for every agile backlog decision.

## Added topics and sources

Principles were checked on 15 November 2026 against the following primary sources. All scenarios, options, and explanatory wording are authored for TeachAPP; no exam-bank questions or source passages were copied.

- `risk-basics`: [PMI — Risk analysis and management](https://www.pmi.org/learning/library/risk-analysis-project-management-7070), covering assessment, response ownership and ongoing review. This is a practice article, not the current exam blueprint.
- `change-control`: [PMI — EVM: A disciplined approach to project management](https://www.pmi.org/learning/library/evm-disciplined-approach-project-management-6003), especially integrated change control and the impact/approval/implementation sequence.
- `scrum-delivery`: [The 2020 Scrum Guide, Ken Schwaber and Jeff Sutherland](https://scrumguides.org/scrum-guide.html), Product Owner, Sprint and Daily Scrum sections. Framework terminology follows this named edition; the scenarios are original and do not reproduce the guide.

Topic IDs and existing stakeholder questions are unchanged so stored history remains meaningful. Every question references one authored key idea; the debrief uses that idea. Existing attempts retain their question and notebook snapshots. Content validation tests check every topic, answer key and idea reference, but they do not establish educational validity or guarantee the model's behavior on new material.

## Original stakeholders demo

`stakeholders-demo` contains three original fictional practice scenarios written for TeachAPP's teaching, notebook, exam, and debrief flow. They are not copied from exam questions, endorsed by PMI, or an official PMP assessment. A score here describes only these three practice questions; it does not indicate certification readiness or full syllabus coverage.

The three learning ideas are identifying affected or influential people, tailoring communication to their needs, and revisiting engagement as the project changes. The basic principles were checked against these PMI Learning Library articles:

- [Engaging Stakeholders for Project Success](https://www.pmi.org/learning/library/engaging-stakeholders-project-success-11199): considering people affected by change and maintaining stakeholder mapping as a project develops.
- [Managing Communications Effectively and Efficiently](https://www.pmi.org/learning/library/managing-communications-effectively-efficiently-5916): matching communication to stakeholder needs and updating communication plans.

The wording and scenarios are original. `requiredIdeaIds` links each question to its debrief idea. The answer key and learning ideas stay on the server; the examinee receives only public questions, choices, and the learner's notebook.
